Before any of this ran, the model was shown thousands of labeled hand photos and adjusted itself until its guesses matched — that is training. Everything else here happens using what it already learned.

Once the model has detected a hand, it does not just say "yes, hand" — it hands back 21 precise (x, y) coordinates for it. Landmarks and Gesture Logic cover what happens next.
