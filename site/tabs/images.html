Images & Colors

A couple of OpenCV gotchas worth knowing before you write this code.

BGR, not RGB

OpenCV loads and draws colors as (B, G, R), not (R, G, B). A "green" circle is (0, 255, 0) either way, but red and blue are swapped from what you'd expect.

Try it — same pixels, different order

These sliders are laid out Blue, Green, Red — to match the order OpenCV actually reads them in, not the Red-Green-Blue order you'd normally expect. Same three numbers per pixel, just reordered.
