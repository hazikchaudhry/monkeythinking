Intro

What Is Computer Vision?

A field of AI that enables computers to interpret and understand visual information from the world — images, video, and more.

Bridges the gap between raw pixels and understanding. Core tasks: image classification, object detection, segmentation, and tracking.

What We're Building

A hand gesture detector that swaps a meme on screen based on how you're pointing.

  • Detects hands using your webcam
  • Recognizes the "pointing" gesture
  • Shows green dots on your hand landmarks
  • Displays a meme that changes based on your gesture
